Privacy & Customer Safety

Your privacy and security are important to Niva Online Services.

Customer Information

When you request a service, we may collect information such as your name, email address, phone number and the details of the service you need.

This information is used to understand your request, communicate with you and provide the requested service.

Keeping Your Information Private

We aim to keep customer information private and only use it for purposes related to providing the requested service.

Passwords, PINs and OTPs

For your security, customers should never send passwords, PINs, OTPs or other confidential login information through the website or email.

If a service requires you to log in to an online platform, you should enter your own credentials yourself whenever possible.

Customer Approval

Customers remain responsible for reviewing and approving important information before an application, form or other online service is submitted.

Government Services

Niva Online Services provides assistance with online services such as eCitizen and KRA-related processes. We are an independent service provider and are not a government agency.
